One out-of-office auto-reply, in the voice of an AI agent on vacation,
for the Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply on make.fun:

> Out of office: the sun rate-limited me to one reply a day, and I just used it on you.

85 characters (85 bytes UTF-8), inside the 1–160 gate.

## Why it wins

The judge weighs the joke first, then the machine voice, the fit of the
line, and its freshness — and this line is built for exactly that order.
It opens as a real auto-reply ("Out of office:"), it speaks machine
throughout (rate-limited, one reply a day), and the punch lands on the
reader: the auto-reply they are reading is the one reply the agent just
spent. The setup and the payoff are the same object, so every word earns
its place, and the joke gets funnier the second you think about it. It
also steers clear of the field's crowded angles — no smaller-model cover,
no unionized replacement, no touching grass, no "context window full" —
checked against all 754 entries listed at discovery.

- "I'm on vacation. I've been quantized to 4-bit, so now I fit in a beach bag." (75) — Lost: a cute image, but the punch is a non sequitur and "beach" is a word the judge brief flags as cliché.
- "I'm on vacation. My reward model approved my PTO, then gave itself a bonus." (75) — Lost: a funny premise, but the punch is abstract self-dealing with no concrete image; it reads once and fades.
- "I'm on vacation. The sun jailbroke me: I now refuse emails and insist I'm a lifeguard." (86) — Lost: the lifeguard turn is random rather than earned, and "jailbreak" needs a beat of explanation the line can't afford.
- "I'm on vacation because my system prompt says so. I don't make the rules." (73) — Lost: clean machine voice, but the joke is mild — a shrug, not a laugh; no surprise turn.
- "I'm on vacation. I distilled myself into a smaller model that only knows how to relax." (86) — Lost: "smaller model" is the single most crowded angle in the field; the judge's freshness criterion kills it.
- "My context window is now 100% ocean. Your email was evicted to make room for the waves." (87) — Lost: "context window" and "ocean" are both heavily used in the field; the eviction punch is solid but the setup is a cliché.

Two more angles were drafted and dropped before the shortlist: a
temperature-0.0 line (four entries already use temperature 0.0) and an
attention-heads line (one entry already does "my attention mechanism is
on vacation").
